About me

I run M.H Import & Export Company, an international trading business covering import, export, wholesale and distribution.

I work with buyers and suppliers on sourcing, supplier management, customs and export documentation, quality inspection, packaging and freight coordination. Our range spans ten categories, from electronics, textiles and machinery to food, cosmetics, perfumes, confectionery and gifts.

My focus is keeping every deal clear and well documented, from the first supplier conversation to delivery.
